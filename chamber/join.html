Membership Levels

NP Membership

A free option for eligible non-profit organisations

Bronze Membership

A starting point for businesses looking to make new connections.

Silver Membership

For businesses interested in greater visibility and training opportunities

Gold Membership

For established businesses seeking additional networking and promotional opportunities.

NP Membership Benefits

  • Free membership for eligible non-profit organizations
  • Access to chamber networking events
  • Business community updates
  • Participation in selected community activities

Bronze Membership Benefits

  • All standard chamber networking benefits
  • Access to selected business workshops
  • Business development resources
  • Discounts on selected chamber events

Silver Membership Benefits

  • All Bronze membership benefits
  • Priority access to selected training sessions
  • Business advertising opportunities
  • Discounts on selected chamber events
  • Eligibility for business spotlights

Gold Membership Benefits

  • All Silver membership benefits
  • Premium business advertising opportunities
  • Priority business spotlight placement
  • Exclusive networking opportunities
  • Priority invitations to special events
  • Premium training opportunities
